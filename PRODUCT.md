# Hot Roster
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User specified Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, React, five Python GenLayer contracts on StudioNet, and Vercel.
## Users
Friends playing fantasy music leagues; reviewers inspecting public replay proof without a wallet.
## Product Purpose
Draft songs, score the Billboard Hot 100, claim artist moments, and resolve disputes against a frozen rulebook.
## Capabilities and Constraints
4–8 players, five songs each, 4–8 Saturday chart dates. Snake draft, one weekly swap, head-to-head standings. Code owns points and money; validators own matching, moment interpretation, and disputes. Optional GEN pools need explicit deposit approval. No public directory, chat, or trading.
## Brand Commitments
Music-chart and sports-broadcast style, bold chart numerals, trading-card rosters, weekly scoreboard, mobile first.
## Evidence on Hand
No completed Hot Roster live proof exists at project creation. Never fabricate receipts or validator outcomes.
