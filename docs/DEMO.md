# Two-minute demo script

Use this script after the proof manifest passes. Until then, present the pending stages accurately; do not narrate missing outcomes as completed. No Portal submission is part of this demo.

**0:00–0:20 — The league.** Open the season without a wallet. “Hot Roster is fantasy football for the Billboard charts, refereed by GenLayer. Four teams drafted five songs for six actual summer 2024 chart weeks.” Show standings and select a team's trading-card roster. Point out the recorded-proof label and week-by-week values.

**0:20–0:40 — Chart judgment.** Open This week, choose the final date and show the rostered chart positions, featured-credit explanation and exact source link. “Python extracted all hundred rows. Validators independently compared the digest and match vector. Code owns scoring; the model only matches songs.” Highlight the No. 1 change and a debut or ten-place climb from actual recorded details.

**0:40–1:00 — Moments.** Show a real awarded card and a rejected card with their quotes. Open the Wikipedia permanent revision or exact archive. “The card and its points were frozen when the league started. Every validator checks the same verdict and finds this passage in its own fetch.”

**1:00–1:20 — Disputes.** Show upheld and overturned live results if both exist. Expand the literal rule clause, evidence quote and correction. “This is an interpretation of a frozen rulebook. An overturned result is rescored by code; a losing challenge forfeits its bond.” If one outcome is missing, disclose that gap and do not claim submission readiness.

**1:20–1:40 — Money and finality.** Show the tiny 40-wei pool, allocation, winner claim and native transfer explorer links. “Pull claims reserve an attempt. Paid means the final native child credited the exact wallet and amount, verified independently on-chain. Simulated StudioNet GEN only.” Briefly show transaction states and appeal timing.

**1:40–2:00 — Build your own.** Open Create a league: dates, card set, editable rulebook, optional fee. Then show the draft room's snake order, taken-song treatment and advisory timer. End at On-chain proof with five deployed addresses, source hashes and receipt links. “Three narrow judgments, five distinct contracts, deterministic scoring and money.”
